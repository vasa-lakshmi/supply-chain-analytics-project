# 🚚 Supply Chain Analytics Project

End-to-end Supply Chain Analytics project using **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to analyze inventory, warehouse, delivery, demand, and profitability data and generate actionable business insights.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

**SwiftOps** is a fictional retail supply chain organization used as the business case for this project.

The organization faced challenges in maintaining optimal inventory levels, reducing delivery delays, controlling operational costs, and improving warehouse efficiency.

This project transforms raw operational data into meaningful business insights using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**.

The analysis focuses on identifying inventory gaps, warehouse performance issues, delivery delays, demand patterns, and profitability differences to support better operational decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

In a supply chain, having too much inventory can increase holding and operational costs, while insufficient inventory can result in stockouts and missed demand.

At the same time, inefficient warehouse operations and longer delivery times can increase costs and affect customer service.

The purpose of this project was to answer questions such as:

- Where are stockout risks highest?
- Which warehouses have longer delivery times?
- Which product categories generate stronger demand?
- Is available inventory sufficient to meet demand?
- Which locations show operational inefficiencies?
- How do operational costs affect profitability?
- Where should management focus improvement efforts?

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focused on the following objectives:

1. Identify warehouses contributing to delivery delays.
2. Detect locations with high stockout risk.
3. Analyze product category performance.
4. Evaluate operational costs and profitability.
5. Compare demand against available inventory.
6. Generate data-driven recommendations to improve supply chain efficiency.

# 🔎 Key Findings

## 1. Inventory Analysis

Demand exceeded available inventory across several product categories.

This indicated potential stockout risk in areas where inventory levels were not sufficient to meet demand.

Certain locations also showed higher stockout risk, highlighting the need for more focused inventory planning.

## 2. Delivery Analysis

Some warehouses recorded longer delivery times than others.

This highlighted differences in warehouse-level delivery performance and helped identify locations that may require further operational investigation.

## 3. Product Category Analysis

Different product categories showed differences in demand and inventory availability.

High-demand categories require closer monitoring to reduce the possibility of inventory shortages.

## 4. Profitability Analysis

The analysis showed differences in operational costs and profitability across locations.

Higher operational costs can negatively affect profitability even when demand remains strong.

This highlights the importance of monitoring both operational costs and business performance together.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

## 1. Improve Replenishment Planning

Prioritize inventory replenishment for high-demand categories and locations with higher stockout risk.

## 2. Monitor Warehouse KPIs

Regularly monitor warehouse-level metrics such as:

- Delivery time
- Inventory availability
- Operational cost
- Profitability

## 3. Investigate Delivery Delays

Warehouses with consistently longer delivery times should be investigated to identify potential process, capacity, or logistics issues.

## 4. Optimize Logistics

Review logistics routes and warehouse-to-location allocation to identify opportunities to reduce delivery time and operational costs.

## 5. Prioritize High-Demand Products

High-demand categories should receive closer inventory monitoring to maintain product availability and reduce stockout risk.

## 6. Control Operational Costs

Warehouses with higher operating costs should be investigated to identify potential efficiency improvements.
